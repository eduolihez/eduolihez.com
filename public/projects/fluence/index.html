How it works

Up and running in minutes

No technical knowledge required. Install, configure, and watch your audience grow.

Download the desktop app for Windows or Mac. Enter your license key and connect your account — set up in under 60 seconds.

2

FAQ

Questions answered.

Is Fluence safe for my Instagram account? +
Fluence uses advanced anti-detection technology (puppeteer-extra stealth) and stays within safe daily limits. It mimics human behaviour with randomised delays. We recommend running it on the same network you normally use Instagram on.
How is this different from a bot? +
Fluence is a desktop growth assistant, not a bot. It runs locally on your computer, uses a real browser, and operates within conservative limits. It never spams or sends bulk messages.
Do I need to keep my computer on? +
Yes. Fluence runs on your desktop and requires the computer to be awake and connected to the internet. It's designed to run overnight or while you work.
Is it really a one-time payment? +
Yes. You pay once and own your license forever. Updates are included. There are no hidden fees, no subscriptions, and no recurring charges.
Can I transfer my license to another computer? +
Each license is tied to one device. If you need to transfer it to a new computer, contact support@fluence.eduolihez.com and we'll handle it within 24 hours.
Which platforms does Fluence support? +
Fluence is available for Windows, macOS (Intel & Apple Silicon) and Linux. Download links are provided after purchase.
What if I want a refund? +
We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee. If Fluence isn't working for you, contact us within 7 days of purchase for a full refund — no questions asked.
Can I upgrade my plan later? +
Yes. You can upgrade from Starter to Growth or Pro at any time. Contact support and we'll apply the difference in price to your upgrade.
