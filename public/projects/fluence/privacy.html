Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 April 2025 · Applies to the Fluence desktop application and website.

Your privacy matters. Fluence is designed to collect as little data as possible. Your Instagram credentials are stored locally on your device and never transmitted to our servers.

1. Who We Are

Fluence is operated by Eduardo Olíhez ("we", "us", "our"), accessible at fluence.eduolihez.com. For any privacy-related enquiries, contact us at privacy@eduolihez.com.

We collect minimal data. The table below describes what we collect, why, and how long we keep it.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Instagram password — stored locally only, never transmitted to us.
  • Instagram session cookies — stored locally on your device only.
  • Personal messages or DMs — we never access your messages.
  • Browsing history or activity outside Fluence — we have no access to this.
  • Payment card details — handled entirely by our payment processor.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect exclusively for:

  • Validating and managing your software license.
  • Preventing license abuse (multiple devices per single license).
  • Improving the Software based on aggregated, anonymised usage patterns.
  • Responding to your support requests.
  • Sending you important product updates (you can opt out at any time).

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes.

5. Data Storage & Security

Session analytics and license data are stored in Google Sheets, hosted on Google's infrastructure in the EU. We apply access controls to ensure only authorised personnel can access this data.

Your Instagram credentials (username, password, cookies) are stored exclusively on your local device using electron-store, an encrypted local storage library. They are never transmitted over the network.

6. Third-Party Services

Fluence uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Workspace — for license validation and analytics storage. Google Privacy Policy →
  • Payment processor — for processing purchases. We do not store payment card data.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format.
  • Objection — object to processing of your data.
  • Restriction — request that we restrict processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@eduolihez.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

The Fluence website (fluence.eduolihez.com) does not use tracking cookies or analytics cookies. We use only essential cookies required for the website to function. No cookie consent banner is required.

9. Children's Privacy

Fluence is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We will notify you of material changes by email if we have your contact details.
