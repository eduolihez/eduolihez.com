PRIVACY POLICY

Exactly what NoWait sends, and exactly what it never does.

LINK BYPASSING

Every shortener page NoWait bypasses is processed locally, in your browser. The destination URL never leaves your device through this mechanism.

WHAT NOWAIT REPORTS

NoWait sends anonymous usage events to its own API (api.eduolihez.com) so its developer can see which bypasses actually get used:

  • Domain only The bare domain of the shortener/tracker being bypassed, e.g. linkvertise.com.
  • Module Which bypass module was triggered for it.
  • Install-only Your browser and the NoWait version, once, when you install or update.

WHAT NOWAIT NEVER SENDS

  • Never The destination URL, its path, or its query string.
  • Never Anything that could reconstruct your browsing history.
  • Never A success/failure verdict — NoWait doesn't verify whether a bypass ultimately worked, only that it ran.
  • Never Any identity attached to the events above.

CROWD BYPASS (OPTIONAL, OFF BY DEFAULT)

If you enable "Crowd Bypass", for shorteners NoWait can't resolve on its own, its URL and discovered destination are sent to the community FastForward crowd-bypass server (NoWait doesn't run its own) so other users don't have to wait either. A hashed IP is kept there to prevent abuse; only its maintainers have access.

LOCAL STORAGE

Your options (whitelist, delay settings, toggles) are stored with chrome.storage.local and never leave your browser.
